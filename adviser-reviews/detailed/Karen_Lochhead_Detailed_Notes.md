I am not keeping Karen under review because she cannot advise — when she speaks to a client, her conversion is among the best on the team (**61%** on contactable/spoken-to clients on **5 March**; **~65%** excluding MPWs/NPWs referenced **19 March**). Protection averages and premium size are competitive.

We keep monitoring because **execution after the appointment** and **CRM truth** lag behind the quality of the advice conversation, and that creates compound risk:

1. **Revenue leakage** — stale leads, unclosed protection cases, and unconverted pipeline (**~£10k** sitting on **19 March**; **~£18k** April insurance in projections flagged as low confidence) never show up in written or banked figures.
2. **MI distortion** — we cannot fairly diagnose lead shortfalls, diary fill, or conversion if **Appointment Booked** limbo and incorrect product status persist; platform nurture from **2 March 2026** depends on correct NPW coding.
3. **Compliance exposure** — by **June** outstanding suitability letters and un-keyed applications dated back to **January**; Friday backlog clearance was not met; Victoria’s fortnightly file reviews and risk-score linkage followed.
4. **Client outcome risk** — protection cases (**Michael Cooper**, **Lucy Muir**, **Hussein**, remortgage clients at **8%** protection penetration) and complex remortgage/purchase timing cases need documented rationale and follow-through.
5. **Target achievability** — higher banking thresholds (**£7,500** / month, **£22,500** / quarter, **£200,000** / year) assume **40** diary opportunities and **30** spoken-to cases per month, Siobhan at **~10 leads/week**, and branch lead quality improving; Karen is sceptical on wider lead delivery and case-mix fairness.
6. **Repeat carry-forward** — the **32-lead / 13-DIP** clean-up agreed **5 March** was still open **19 March** with a **26 March** deadline missed; same pattern on reports and protection list actions **22 May**.

Handover requires the next manager to inherit context, not just a KPI dashboard.
